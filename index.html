< About Me />

Results-driven QA Automation Engineer with 8.1 years of experience delivering quality across high-throughput fintech platforms, healthcare systems, travel, HRMS, and LMS applications. I lead QA for a scalable Connected Banking platform processing 1.6M+ daily transactions across a 57-service distributed architecture — validated at ~78.5 TPS against an 80 TPS target with 99.9% uptime objective.

My expertise spans the full testing spectrum — Functional Manual Testing, API Testing, UI Automation, Performance Testing, Integration Testing, and AI-driven quality engineering. I specialise in Playwright (TypeScript), Selenium WebDriver (Java), REST Assured, and Postman, with CI/CD integration via Jenkins and test management through JIRA, TestRail, and Zephyr. I have hands-on experience working in Agile/Scrum environments, collaborating closely with developers, product managers, and AI/ML teams to ensure high-quality, on-time releases.

What sets me apart is my hands-on experience at the intersection of AI and QA. I have tested Live AI Chatbots, validated AI-driven Dispute Management flows, anomaly detection models, fraud alert systems, and AI-suggested resolutions — a rare and in-demand skill set in today's quality engineering landscape.

Career Goal: To grow as a Senior QA Engineer, QA Lead, or SDET — leveraging AI-driven testing, automation at scale, and performance validation to drive quality engineering excellence in complex distributed systems and fintech platforms.
