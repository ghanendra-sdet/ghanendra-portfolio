AI Dispute Resolution Engine

The single shared AI support layer behind all six Paywize products — resolving ~80% of disputes without a human agent, and cutting average ticket resolution from a 24–72 hour baseline to under 6 hours.

Overview

Every product — Collection, Payout, Connected Banking, BBPS, Reseller, and YOBO — generates support load: stuck transactions, account-detail changes, commission questions. Instead of six separate chatbots, this engine centralizes dispute handling into one shared AI layer all six products route into, so a regression in intent recognition can't silently degrade support quality in just one corner of the platform.

In plain terms, this is the platform's single support brain — a merchant's issue is routed here regardless of which product it originated from, classified, and either resolved conversationally or hard-escalated to a human with full context. The engine itself is a smaller, focused set of AI-specific services sitting on top of the shared platform:

How It Works & My Role

Core Resolution Flow

  1. An issue is raised from any of the six connected products
  2. Intent Recognition classifies it: Transaction Status, Email/Mobile Change, Onboarding, Commission Dispute, or General Q&A
  3. AI resolves confidently (~80% of cases) and closes the ticket
  4. Low-confidence or security-sensitive cases escalate to a human agent with full context
  5. Ticket closed — average under 6 hours

Why Cross-Product Consistency Is the Central Risk

Because this engine is shared, a regression doesn't stay contained to one product — an intent-recognition bug found via one product's tickets can silently degrade support quality across all six connected products simultaneously.

Challenges & Problem-Solving

Two representative problems from this module — how they were found and resolved, not just what the final test covered.

One product silently resolving support tickets at a lower rate
Challenge

BBPS-originated Transaction Status queries were resolving at a measurably lower rate than the other five products, despite the AI logic being identical across all of them.

Approach

Compared the raw transaction-data payload format across all six products instead of assuming the AI model itself was at fault.

Outcome

Filed as BUG-AID-5012 — traced to a BBPS-specific field format the intent handler didn't parse correctly, a data-normalization fix rather than a model fix.

A sensitive change applying despite an abandoned verification
Challenge

A Mobile Number Change request applied the change even though the user had abandoned the conversation before verification completed.

Approach

Tested the specific sequence of starting verification and closing the session mid-flow, rather than only testing the fully-completed happy path.

Outcome

Filed as BUG-AID-5031 (Blocker); the fix gated the actual field update on a confirmed verification event, not just conversation progress.

Key Achievements

  • Reduced average ticket resolution time from a 24–72 hour baseline to under 6 hours through AI-driven automated resolution
  • Validated ~80% of raised issues resolved directly by the AI agent, without human involvement, across all six issue categories
  • Validated intent recognition, response accuracy, and context retention across dispute management conversational flows
  • Designed a dedicated escalation-integrity test for the Commission/Revenue Dispute category, ensuring adjustment requests always route to a human agent
  • Conducted negative/boundary testing on AI responses to confirm graceful degradation and reliable human-agent fallback
