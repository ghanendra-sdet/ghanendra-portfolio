Overview

Connected Banking lets a merchant link their own bank account directly to the platform, transact through it, and view balances/statements in real time. It's part of an 11+ module, 57-service platform validated under sustained load — a core testing challenge is that a transaction attempted while whitelisting is still pending must be blocked cleanly, never silently allowed or silently stuck.

In plain terms, Connected Banking turns a merchant's own bank account into a first-class part of the platform — once linked and whitelisted, it behaves like a native wallet rather than an external account the merchant has to manage separately. Underneath the UI, the module is composed of its own ~29-service architecture on top of the shared 57-service platform:

How It Works & My Role

Core Connected Banking Flow

  1. Merchant signs up and adds a bank account
  2. Account enters Pending Whitelisting until Admin approval
  3. Once whitelisted, the merchant can recharge via Bank Widget or Ledger Widget
  4. Merchant initiates transactions through the connected account
  5. Mini Statement & Reports reflect balances and transaction history in real time

Challenges & Problem-Solving

Two representative problems from this module — how they were found and resolved, not just what the final test covered.

A whitelisting-state race only visible under real load
Challenge

Transactions attempted during the Pending Whitelisting window needed to be blocked, but a race between whitelisting completion and transaction submission could only be observed under real concurrent load, not single-user functional tests.

Approach

Extended JMeter/Grafana load testing to specifically fire transaction attempts during active whitelisting transitions, not just after whitelisting completed.

Outcome

Validated the block held even at 100 TPS peak, across 405,000+ transactions in a single sustained load test.

Commercial/fee wallet slab boundaries
Challenge

The commercial/fee wallet model uses slab-based fee boundaries — validating correct fee application exactly at a slab threshold required precise test data, not representative samples.

Approach

Built a boundary-value test matrix around every slab threshold and the insufficient-balance blocking condition specifically.

Outcome

Confirmed slab logic and insufficient-balance blocking behaved correctly end-to-end, including at exact threshold values.
