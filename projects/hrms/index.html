Overview

Staffiq streamlines HR operations — payroll compliance (PF, ESI, TDS), attendance, recruitment, performance management, and biometric integration — under role-based access control. Its Employee Self-Service (ESS/MyInfo) module is where an employee manages their own personal details, and where a field's access level (HR-controlled vs. employee-editable) determines whether it's a usability feature or a data-integrity risk.

In plain terms, Staffiq is the company's system of record for its own workforce — HR configures what every employee sees, and the ESS module is the narrow, employee-facing window into that record. Underneath the ESS form, the platform is composed of these core modules:

How It Works & My Role

Core ESS Flow

  1. Employee logs in to the ESS / MyInfo portal
  2. Personal Details form loads with field-access control applied per field
  3. Employee edits only the fields they're permitted to (nickname, marital status, etc.)
  4. HR-controlled fields (DOB, Employee ID) remain read-only and feed payroll & compliance
  5. Save triggers a confirmation and persists the update

My Role & Responsibilities

  • Designed and executed manual test cases covering functional, regression, smoke, and sanity testing
  • Conducted manual API testing (Postman) with direct SQL database validation
  • Validated field-level GUI behavior — enabled/disabled state, control-type correctness — across every field
  • Validated file upload constraints (accepted formats, size limits) for profile picture uploads

Why Field-Access Control Is the Central Risk

HR-controlled fields like Date of Birth feed payroll and compliance — a field that's accidentally left employee-editable is a data-integrity risk, not just a UI bug, since it's filled out repeatedly by every employee in the company.

Challenges & Problem-Solving

Two representative problems from this module — how they were found and resolved, not just what the final test covered.

An HR-only field left editable by the employee
Challenge

The Date of Birth field — meant to be HR-controlled — was editable by the ESS employee, with changes saving successfully with no HR approval step at all.

Approach

Built a field-by-field enabled/disabled verification pass covering all 14 Personal Details fields, rather than spot-checking a subset.

Outcome

Filed as BUG-HRM-7021 (Critical); reinforced enforcing field-access control at both the form-rendering and save/API layers, not the UI alone.

A stated file-size limit that wasn't actually enforced
Challenge

Profile picture uploads over the stated 1 MB limit were still being accepted, contradicting the documented constraint.

Approach

Traced the gap to a size check that validated file extension but never actually checked file size.

Outcome

Filed as BUG-HRM-7038; the fix added an explicit server-side file-size check as the authoritative validation, not just a client-side one.
